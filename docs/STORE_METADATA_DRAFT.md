# Reclaim v1 store metadata draft

This is working copy for the first iPhone + Android beta/public listing. Keep claims aligned with the shipped product; do not add clinical or guaranteed cessation claims.

## Shared identity

**App name:** Reclaim

**Positioning:** A private behavior-support and tracking app for adults who want to quit smoking, smoke less, or understand their smoking patterns.

**Core promise:** Give users a calmer place to log honestly, handle cravings, see progress, and act on patterns without shame-based messaging.

## Apple App Store draft

**Name:** Reclaim

**Subtitle (30 characters max):** Quit smoking. Reclaim life.

**Promotional text (170 characters max):**

Track cravings, understand patterns, build supportive routines, and see what you are reclaiming—one honest day at a time.

**Description:**

Reclaim is a private smoking behavior-support app built around one idea: your next decision matters more than perfection.

Choose the journey that fits you:
• Quit now — track smoke-free time, money reclaimed, cigarettes avoided, recovery milestones, cravings, and goals.
• Smoke less — set a reduction target, log honestly, and see daily progress without treating an unlogged day as a success.
• Understand my smoking — track what happens first, then use patterns and insights to decide what you want to change.

When a craving hits, Reclaim gives you short coping tools and lets you record what helped. Daily check-ins add context, while Insights only surface patterns when your logs support them.

Reclaim also includes:
• Personalized Today guidance based on your recent activity
• Savings goals tied to money reclaimed from smoking
• Source-backed health-recovery milestones for quit journeys
• A learning library
• Optional reminders
• Community support with reporting and blocking controls
• An iPhone home-screen widget
• In-app privacy information and permanent account deletion

Reclaim is a behavior-support and tracking product. It is not a medical provider and does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical advice.

## Google Play draft

**App name (30 characters max):** Reclaim

**Short description (80 characters max):**

Quit, reduce, or understand smoking with cravings, insights, goals and support.

**Full description:**

Reclaim is a private smoking behavior-support and tracking app for adults who want to quit smoking, smoke less, or understand their smoking patterns.

Choose your pace instead of forcing one path.

QUIT NOW
See smoke-free time, money reclaimed, cigarettes avoided, recovery milestones, cravings, and savings goals.

SMOKE LESS
Set a reduction target and log honestly. Reclaim shows the day as it is and never treats a missing log as proof of success.

UNDERSTAND MY SMOKING
Track first. Reclaim can then surface useful patterns from the information you choose to log.

CRAVING SUPPORT
Open short coping tools when an urge hits and record what helped. Reclaim can use previous tool feedback to make the support screen more useful over time.

PERSONALIZED TODAY
The Today screen uses recent check-ins, cravings, progress, and goals to decide what is most useful to put one tap away.

INSIGHTS THAT LEAD SOMEWHERE
When Reclaim has enough evidence for a pattern, the insight includes a relevant next action instead of ending as a static chart.

MORE WAYS TO SUPPORT YOUR JOURNEY
• Daily mood check-ins
• Savings goals
• Source-backed quit recovery milestones
• Learning content
• Optional reminders
• Community interactions with reporting and blocking controls
• Account deletion from inside the app

Reclaim is designed around agency and honest progress, not shame or perfection.

## Google Play health declaration note

Reclaim clearly has health-related functionality. Do **not** declare that the app has no health features.

Based on Google's current declaration categories, the closest fit is **Mental and Behavioral Health**, because Google explicitly includes addiction recovery programs in that category. Reconfirm the available categories in Play Console at submission time and declare every category that accurately matches the final shipped feature set.

The public privacy policy must remain active and linked both in Play Console and inside the app.

## Copy guardrails

Do not use claims such as:
- "guaranteed to help you quit"
- "clinically proven" unless Reclaim later has evidence supporting that exact claim
- "treats nicotine addiction"
- diagnostic or treatment language
- unverified health outcome percentages
- fake testimonials or rankings

Do use:
- "behavior support"
- "tracking"
- "patterns from your logs"
- "source-backed milestones"
- "optional reminders"
- "not medical advice"
